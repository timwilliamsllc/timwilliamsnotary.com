What You Need Before Your Session

Please review the following requirements to ensure a smooth and successful Remote Online Notarization (RON) session:

  • Valid Government‑Issued Photo ID – Driver’s license, passport, or state ID.
  • Stable Internet Connection – A wired or strong Wi‑Fi connection is recommended.
  • Device with Camera & Microphone – Laptop, desktop, tablet, or smartphone.
  • Quiet, Well‑Lit Location – So your identity can be verified clearly.
  • Documents Ready to Upload – PDF format preferred.
  • U.S. Location – You must be physically located within the United States during the session.

Before We Begin

Once you join the session, you will complete identity verification and upload your documents. Please ensure all pages are clear and readable.

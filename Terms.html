Service Requirements

  • You must provide a valid, unexpired government‑issued photo ID.
  • You must be physically located within the United States during the notarization.
  • You must have a device with a working camera, microphone, and stable internet connection.
  • You must complete identity verification as required by Missouri law.

Electronic Records & Signatures

By using Remote Online Notary services, you consent to the use of electronic signatures, electronic records, and digital notarization seals as permitted under Missouri law.

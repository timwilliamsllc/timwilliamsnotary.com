2. Eligibility

To use RON services, you must:

  • Be physically located within the United States during the notarization session.
  • Provide a valid, government‑issued photo ID.
  • Use a device with a working camera, microphone, and stable internet connection.

All signers must complete identity verification as required by Missouri law and the BlueNotary platform. Failure to pass verification will prevent the notarization from proceeding.

5. Document Requirements

Documents must be clear, readable, and ready for upload before the session. Illegible or incomplete documents may delay or prevent notarization.

6. Prohibited Documents

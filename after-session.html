What Happens After Your Session

Once your Remote Online Notarization (RON) session is complete, your notarized documents will be processed and delivered securely through the BlueNotary platform.

Receiving Your Documents

  • Your notarized documents will be available for download immediately after the session.
  • You will receive an email notification with access to your completed documents.
  • All documents are stored securely within your BlueNotary account.

Need Additional Copies?

You may download your documents at any time. If you need assistance retrieving them, feel free to reach out.

Services

I provide Remote Online Notary (RON) services for Missouri signers and documents that can be notarized under Missouri law using an approved online platform.

Common document types

  • General acknowledgments
  • Jurats and sworn statements
  • Affidavits and declarations
  • Powers of attorney (where permitted)
  • Financial and banking forms
  • Business and employment documents
  • Other documents that require notarization under Missouri law

If you are unsure whether your document can be notarized online, you may email a brief description of the document (no sensitive details) before scheduling.

How a typical session works

  1. You schedule your session using the secure online booking link.
  2. You upload your document and complete identity verification.
  3. We meet in a secure audio‑video session to review and notarize your document.
  4. You receive your completed, notarized document electronically.

All sessions are recorded and stored in compliance with Missouri RON requirements.

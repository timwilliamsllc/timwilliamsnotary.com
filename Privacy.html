1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Tim Williams LLC collects, uses, and protects your information during Remote Online Notarization (RON) services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, address, date of birth)
  • Government‑issued ID for identity verification
  • Uploaded documents for notarization
  • Session recordings as required by Missouri law
  • Technical information such as device type and connection quality

3. How Your Information Is Used

  • To verify your identity
  • To complete your notarization
  • To comply with Missouri RON legal requirements
  • To maintain secure records of notarized documents

4. How Your Information Is Protected

All information is stored securely through the BlueNotary platform, which uses encryption and industry‑standard security protocols. Session recordings and journal entries are retained as required by Missouri law.

5. Sharing of Information

Your information is never sold or shared except:

  • When required by law
  • When necessary to complete your notarization
  • When requested by you in writing

6. Your Rights

You may request copies of your notarized documents at any time through the BlueNotary platform.

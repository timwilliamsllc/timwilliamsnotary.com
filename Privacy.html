Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Tim Williams LLC collects, uses, and protects your information when you use Remote Online Notary (RON) services. By scheduling or participating in a notarization session, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Full legal name
  • Government‑issued identification details
  • Contact information (email, phone number)
  • Audio and video recordings required by Missouri RON law
  • Documents you upload for notarization
  • Identity verification results

How Your Information Is Used

  • To verify your identity as required by Missouri law
  • To complete your notarization session
  • To generate legally compliant electronic records
  • To deliver your notarized documents securely
  • To maintain required audit trails and session logs

How Your Information Is Protected

All notarization sessions are conducted through the secure BlueNotary platform, which uses encryption and industry‑standard security practices. Tim Williams LLC does not sell or share your information with third parties except as required by law.

Retention of Records

Missouri law requires Remote Online Notaries to retain electronic journals and audio‑video recordings for a legally mandated period. These records are stored securely and accessed only when required for legal or regulatory purposes.

Your Rights

  • You may request a copy of your notarized documents at any time.
  • You may request clarification about how your information is used.
  • You may request corrections to your contact information.

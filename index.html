Pricing

My pricing is simple, transparent, and aligned with the standard Remote Online Notarization (RON) fees used by BlueNotary.

Standard RON Fees

  • $25 — First notarized document
  • $10 — Technology fee (secure platform, ID verification, recording)
  • $10 — Each additional signer
  • $10 — Each additional document

Optional

  • $20 — After‑hours session (only applied if enabled in BlueNotary)

These fees cover all standard online notarization needs. The platform will automatically calculate the correct total during your session.

Requirements

To complete a Remote Online Notarization session, you will need:

  • A valid, unexpired government‑issued photo ID
  • A device with a camera (computer, tablet, or smartphone)
  • Stable internet connection
  • The document(s) you need notarized

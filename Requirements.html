Requirements for Remote Online Notarization

To complete a Remote Online Notary (RON) session, you must meet certain identification, technology, and document requirements. Reviewing these ahead of time helps ensure a smooth, efficient appointment.

Identification

  • A valid, unexpired government‑issued photo ID (driver’s license, passport, etc.).
  • The name on your ID must match the name on your document.
  • You must be able to clearly present your ID on camera during the session.

Technology

  • A computer, tablet, or smartphone with a working camera and microphone.
  • A stable internet connection capable of supporting live video.
  • Ability to access the secure online notarization platform via browser or app.

Documents

  • Your document must be complete and ready for signature (no blank required fields).
  • You must upload a clear, readable copy of the document to the platform.
  • The document must be eligible for notarization under Missouri law.

Location

You may be located outside Missouri at the time of signing. The notarization is performed under Missouri law because the notary is commissioned in Missouri.
